Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Privacy Policy

CodeConnect Media is committed to complete transparency, client data integrity, and strict adherence to global privacy laws and Google advertising standards.

Last Updated: January 2026 • Entity: CodeConnect Media • Ludhiana, India

01. Introduction & Scope

CodeConnect Media ("we," "us," or "our") is a digital technology and software development agency headquartered in Ludhiana, Punjab, India, serving clients across India, the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and worldwide.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, process, store, and safeguard your personal and business data when you visit our website (codeconnectmedia.com), submit project consultation inquiries, use our custom software/ERP solutions, or interact with platforms we develop and operate (including third-party platforms such as Trainbooking.store).

02. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide software architecture, web development, UI/UX design, and digital marketing services:

Identity & Contact

Your full name, company name, corporate email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, and country of residence.

Technical & Project

IP address, browser type, device specs, scope documents, technical wireframes, and API credentials shared under NDA.

Billing Details

Invoicing addresses, tax IDs (GST/VAT), and payment status via Stripe. We do not store raw credit card numbers.

03. How We Use Your Information

  • Delivering Engineering Services: To build, deploy, test, and maintain custom software, CRM/ERP systems, e-commerce storefronts, and web platforms.
  • Client Communication: To conduct sprint reviews, send architectural updates, and coordinate deliverables via Slack, Zoom, Google Meet, and email.
  • Google Ads & Marketing Optimization: To deliver compliant advertising campaigns and track conversion velocity without invasive personal profiling.
  • Invoicing & Compliance: To issue commercial tax invoices, manage Stripe international payments (USD/GBP/EUR), and comply with legal requirements.

04. International Billing & Stripe Processing

For clients based in the United States, UK, Europe, and Canada, all credit card and ACH transactions are securely processed via Stripe.

CodeConnect Media does not hold or store client credit card numbers or banking secrets on our servers. All transactions are protected by Stripe's PCI-DSS Level 1 certified security infrastructure.

05. Third-Party Portals & Travel Platforms

CodeConnect Media engineers and operates specialized web platforms, including authentic third-party travel assistance portals like Trainbooking.store.

Travel Platform Privacy Standards: Any user data collected on travel booking engines is utilized exclusively for generating reservations, dispatching SMS/email booking confirmations, and processing authorized transaction fees. We never resell passenger or traveler information to data brokers.

06. Google Ads & Cookie Policies

We use Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion tags. These tools place lightweight, anonymous cookies in your browser to analyze traffic trends and measure advertising efficacy. You may disable cookies at any time via your browser settings without affecting the core functionality of our site.

07. Your Rights & Data Security

All project repositories, client data, and staging environments are guarded by 256-bit SSL encryption, SSH access keys, and two-factor authentication.

You have the right to request access to, update, or permanently delete any personal data we hold. To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@codeconnectmedia.com.
